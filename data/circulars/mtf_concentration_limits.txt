MTF SCRIP-LEVEL CONCENTRATION LIMITS
Per SEBI and exchange guidelines, no single client should exceed the following
in their MTF position for any one scrip:

Nifty50 scrips: maximum 50% of approved MTF limit in a single scrip
BSE200 (non-Nifty50) scrips: maximum 30% of approved limit
BSE500 (non-BSE200) scrips: maximum 20% of approved limit
Scrips not on any approved index: maximum 10% of approved limit

FAMILY-GROUP LIMITS
Related accounts (family codes mapped in the EXCEPTION.xlsx file) are aggregated.
The family group combined exposure in any single scrip must not exceed:
- Nifty50 scrips: ₹5 crore
- BSE200 scrips: ₹3 crore
- Other approved: ₹1 crore
Exceeding family limits triggers immediate email alert to all zone RMS heads.

ZONE DISTRIBUTION
Mastertrust operates four zones for MTF reporting:
ZODELH (Delhi), ZOLUDH (Ludhiana), ZOCHND (Chandigarh), ZOMUMB (Mumbai)
Each zone head receives a separate concentration report for their clients only.
ZOMT and ZOCDR are combined in the CDR + MT report.

CONCENTRATION PIPELINE PROCESS
1. Download Nifty50, BSE200, BSE500 index files (INDEX_FILE_DOWNLOAD)
2. Download KORP shortage file
3. Run concentration compute: reads positions, applies tier rules, checks family limits
4. Output: violation rows with zone, client, scrip, current %, limit %
5. Emails are sent zone-wise with only that zone's violations
6. Files are backed up and original position files are deleted
